What the mark says

The cubeA contained system with three faces: the input that comes in, the policy that checks it and the evidence that is kept.

The lineIt stays armed above the cube, and its tip glows to show the tripwire is live. The node at the centre vertex is where an agent’s action is judged.

The motionActions travel in along the cube’s edges to the centre. The node inspects them, the cube flinches and a light passes over its faces. Then a line runs out to the corner and a seal stamps it: the receipt.
