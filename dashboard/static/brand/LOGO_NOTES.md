# Tripline logo

Concept: a T made of two ideas.
- The horizontal bar is the boundary (the line an action must not cross).
- The red stroke above it is the action, stopped short of the line.
- The stem below is a receipt with a torn edge: the proof that is left behind.
- The red dot on the first "i" in the wordmark echoes the stopped action.

Colours: ink #0F1115, paper #FFFFFF, accent (decisions only) #E5484D.
Wordmark: Inter Display SemiBold (SIL Open Font License), converted to outlines, so no font install is needed.
Files: mark-dark / mark-light (app icon style), mark-bare (no tile), lockup-on-light / lockup-on-dark, favicon.svg,
PNG exports (mark 512, favicon 32, apple-touch 180, lockups).
Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the red stroke. Do not recolour the red stroke.
