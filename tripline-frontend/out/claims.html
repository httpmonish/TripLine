Acknowledged Honest Limits

Security systems with unstated assumptions create false assurance. Tripline documents its known architectural residuals explicitly.

Exact Substring Canary Scan

Egress canary scan does not detect Base64-encoded, reversed, or fragmented canary tokens across comments.

// source:docs/limitations.md & docs/phase3_layer_attribution.md Sec. 3

Decision Proof Replay Window

Decision proof tokens minted by ArmorIQ carry a 60-second TTL. Replay within the active 60s window is permitted.

// source:mcp/common/proof.py & docs/JUDGE_QA.md Q9

Local Witness Key Custody

Witness key is stored locally in .tripline/witness_key.txt. HMAC detects modification of covered fields, but key holder could forge entries.
